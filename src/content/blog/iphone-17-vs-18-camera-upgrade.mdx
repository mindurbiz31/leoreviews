## What are new features in iPhone 18 Camera?

The most significant camera change between the iPhone 17 and rumored iPhone 18 may be a small one: **the aperture**. The iPhone 17 is expected to have a static aperture, so its lens opening cannot change; it must rely heavily on computational photography to adapt to different lighting. The iPhone 18, by contrast, is rumored to introduce a dynamic aperture that physically adjusts how much light reaches the sensor.

## What are the benefits of dynamic aperture in iPhone 18?

Dynamic aperture will give iPhone 18 more control over exposure, depth of field, and motion in challenging lighting. It will also help photographers create more natural background blur or reduce light in bright scenes with less reliance on computational processing. The change may seem minor, but if Apple implements it well, it could make the iPhone 18 camera noticeably more versatile than its predecessor.

## In low-light photography, which iPhone will work well, iPhone 17 or 18?

In low-light photography, the iPhone 18 Pro will have a meaningful hardware advantage over the iPhone 17. The iPhone 17 relies on its fixed wide aperture (f/1.6) and Apple’s computational photography to brighten dark scenes, reduce noise, and recover shadow detail. By contrast, the iPhone 18 Pro’s **rumored f/1.48 wide dynamic (f/1.48 to f/4.0) aperture** will let more light reach the sensor before computational processing begins. In practice, that will result in cleaner shadows, finer detail, and less aggressive noise reduction in night photos.

## How wide dynamic aperture helps low-light photography in iPhone 18?

iPhone 18 comes with dynamic wide aperture having range of f/1.48 to f/4.0, while iPhone 17 comes with static aperture having fixed size f/1.6. Previous version iPhone that is 17, it depends on Apple’s computational photography to brighten dark scenes, reduce noise, and recover shadow detail. But in newer version of iPhone, the iPhone 18 can start with **more real light**, giving its image processing a higher-quality source to work with. The wider aperture may also preserve natural textures that can sometimes appear overly processed in smartphone night modes.

## How much more powerful is the lens of iPhone 18 than iPhone 17?

When it comes to zoom and distance shooting, the iPhone 18 Pro could have a clear advantage over the iPhone 17, particularly for subjects that are far away. The iPhone 17 relies on a 2x optical-quality crop from its 48MP sensor, which can produce surprisingly detailed results at moderate distances, but beyond that it increasingly depends on digital processing, with digital zoom extending up to 10x. The iPhone 18 Pro, meanwhile, is expected to offer a dedicated telephoto system capable of delivering up to **8x optical-quality zoom**, giving it a much stronger foundation for capturing distant subjects without sacrificing as much fine detail.

## In real world use, which camera will perform better, iPhone 18 or 17?

In real-world use, the difference should be most noticeable when photographing concerts, sports, architecture, wildlife, or people from across a street. Rather than simply enlarging pixels, the iPhone 18 Pro’s longer optical reach could preserve better texture, edge definition, and subject separation at higher magnifications. For casual snapshots, the iPhone 17’s 2x crop remains perfectly capable, but for serious distance shooting, the iPhone 18 Pro is shaping up to be the considerably more versatile camera.

## What is new in iPhone 18’s front camera for selfie people?

On the front-camera side, the iPhone 17 and iPhone 18 Pro are expected to be much closer, with both featuring an **18MP Center Stage front camera** designed to keep subjects naturally framed during selfies, video calls, and group shots.
