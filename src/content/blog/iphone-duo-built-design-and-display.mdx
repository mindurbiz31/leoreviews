## Grade 5 Titanium: Aerospace Technology in Your Hand

The **iPhone Duo** frame is crafted from **Grade 5 Titanium**—an advanced alloy composed of titanium, aluminum, and vanadium. Heavily utilized in aerospace and high-performance automotive applications, Grade 5 titanium boasts one of the highest strength-to-weight ratios of any metal.

As the icing on the cake, a fine brushed finish reduces visible fingerprint smudges compared to high-gloss stainless steel, giving the outer band a subtle matte texture that resists micro-scratches.

## Display: Full Specs, Features, and Real-World Usability

### 5.4-inch Outer Display

When folded, the device retains a compact, pocketable format featuring a **5.4-inch outer OLED display**. Boasting a **1398 × 2034 resolution** at an ultra-dense **460 ppi**, it offers standard iPhone text clarity and color accuracy protected by a Ceramic Shield cover.

At **460 PPI** (Pixels Per Inch), a single linear inch packs 460 pixels—equaling over **211,600 pixels per square inch** ($460 \times 460$). Individual pixels are imperceptible to the naked eye at standard viewing distances, delivering razor-sharp text and crisp images.

### 7.6-inch Inner Display for Power Users

Unfolding the device reveals a expansive **7.6-inch inner foldable OLED display** running at **1878 × 2670 resolution** (**430 ppi**).

Both the inner and outer displays feature an etched **nano-texture finish** directly integrated into the outer polymer layer. Unlike traditional glossy glass, nano-texture scatters incoming ambient light to minimize glare and reflections while significantly masking the center folding crease.

## Advanced Display Technologies

### ProMotion Variable Refresh Rate

Most standard smartphone displays update at **60Hz** (60 times per second). iPhone Duo features **ProMotion**, an adaptive variable refresh rate system:

- **Dynamic Performance:** Scales smoothly up to **120Hz** when swiping, gaming, or scrolling.
- **Battery Efficiency:** Automatically throttles down as low as **1Hz** when viewing static pages or text, ramping back to 120Hz the instant your finger touches the screen.

### 3,000 Nits Outdoor Peak Brightness

The display is capable of pushing local highlighted areas up to **3,000 nits**, allowing clear visibility even under direct sunlight.

> **What is a Nit?**
> A nit measures luminance—the amount of visible light emitted per square meter cd/m². A **3,000-nit outdoor peak brightness** rating means the panel can output 3,000 candelas per square meter when handling HDR highlights or combating extreme outdoor ambient glare.
